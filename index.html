FAQ

Common questions before starting.

Helpful details for individuals, proprietors, and businesses planning a filing or compliance review.

Do you handle both personal and business tax returns?

Yes. The firm supports salaried individuals, professionals, proprietorships, partnerships, and small companies.

Can you help with a GST notice?

Yes. Share the notice, portal details, and available records so the response path can be reviewed.

What documents are usually needed for ITR?

Form 16, AIS/TIS, bank statements, investment proofs, capital gain details, and business records where applicable.

Can new businesses get registration support?

Yes. Registration and first compliance steps can be planned together for a cleaner launch.
